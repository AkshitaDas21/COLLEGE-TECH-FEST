Frequently Asked Questions

❓ When is TechFest 2026?

TechFest 2026 will be held on March 15-17, 2026 at the Central Convention Center.

❓ How much is the registration fee?

Registration fees vary by category: Students: $50, Professionals: $150, Entrepreneurs: $200. Early bird discounts available!

❓ Can I get a refund?

Yes, refunds are available up to 2 weeks before the event. Please contact our support team for details.

❓ Is accommodation included?

Accommodation is not included in the registration fee but can be added during registration at a discounted rate.

❓ Can I bring a guest?

Each registration is for one person. If your guest wants to attend, they'll need to register separately.

❓ Will there be online attendance?

Yes! We offer hybrid attendance. Register for virtual access to live-stream all keynotes and selected sessions.

❓ How do I become a sponsor?

Interested in sponsoring? Email us at sponsorship@techfest2026.com with your details and we'll send you sponsorship packages.

❓ Can I speak at TechFest?

We welcome speaker proposals! Submit your topic to speakers@techfest2026.com. We'll review and get back to you.
